# Making AI work for you: Minimising AI slop on Drupal projects

A talk by Alex Skrypnyk on the practices and tools that keep AI-assisted Drupal work clean, tested and owned by the developer. Every practice in the talk names the kind of AI slop it prevents.

## Where it was presented

- **Event:** Melbourne Drupal Meetup
- **Date:** July 2026
