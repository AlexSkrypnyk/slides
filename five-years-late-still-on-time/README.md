# Five Years Late, Still On Time: mentalhealthonline.org.au Drupal 8 to 11 journey

A talk by Alex Skrypnyk on upgrading mentalhealthonline.org.au from Drupal 8 to Drupal 11.

## Where it was presented

- **Event:** DrupalSouth Community Day, Canberra
- **Date:** November 2025
- **Recording:** [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Lc4n4goSSvw)

## Slides

- [Open the deck online](https://alexskrypnyk.github.io/slides/five-years-late-still-on-time/).
- To present from a local copy, open [index.html](index.html) in a browser and press <kbd>F</kbd> for fullscreen. The arrow keys move between slides.
- [five-years-late-still-on-time.pdf](five-years-late-still-on-time.pdf) is the PDF export.

The deck was built in Google Slides. `slides/` holds 1 image per slide exported from it. `index.html` shows those images in order.
